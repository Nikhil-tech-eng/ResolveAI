# Contact and Escalation Policy

1. Keep the customer in the current supported conversation channel and verify their account before discussing private details.
2. Escalate fraud, legal, and data-privacy requests to their specialist queues without attempting an automatic resolution.
3. Escalate when confidence is low, a policy-blocked action is disputed, a strongly negative unresolved issue has had two attempts, three troubleshooting steps have failed, or an SLA is at risk.
4. Create or update a human-ready ticket with verified customer, order, investigation, and action-log details.
5. State that the case is being handed to a specialist; do not promise a contact method or resolution time not present in the verified records.
