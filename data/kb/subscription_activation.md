# Subscription Activation

1. Verify the customer and read the subscription status recorded on the account.
2. Confirm payment using the available order and payment records; do not infer payment from a subscription label alone.
3. If payment is recorded but the subscription is not active, document the activation or synchronization mismatch.
4. Do not mark the subscription active or charge the customer again without an authorized action and verified records.
5. Escalate the sync failure to subscription support and tell the customer that the status is under review.
