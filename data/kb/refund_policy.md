# Refund Policy

1. Confirm the customer and order using the read tools; do not rely on an order number supplied without lookup.
2. Check the order payment status and payment logs. A refund is allowed only when the order is paid or a payment record confirms that money was deducted.
3. If neither condition is true, explain the recorded payment facts and do not initiate a refund.
4. For an eligible order, use the refund action tool and report its result. Do not promise a settlement date that the payment records do not provide.
5. Log the outcome and escalate if the customer disputes a policy decision or the payment evidence is inconsistent.
