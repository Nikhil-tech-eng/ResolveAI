# Damaged Item Workflow

1. Confirm the customer, order, and delivered status from the support records.
2. Ask the customer to identify the affected item and describe the damage; request supporting evidence through the approved support channel when needed.
3. Do not mark damage as verified until the evidence has been reviewed and recorded by an authorized support process.
4. After verification, schedule a replacement or check the return policy before creating a return request.
5. If the order or evidence cannot be confirmed, explain what is missing and escalate instead of promising a replacement or refund.
