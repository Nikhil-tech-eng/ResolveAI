# Shipping Delays

1. Verify the order and inspect its shipment status, carrier, tracking number, and delivery date.
2. Compare the current tracking information with the carrier's recorded updates; do not guess a new delivery date.
3. Explain the latest verified status and provide the tracking reference when available.
4. If the shipment is marked delayed or has no useful update, record the issue and route it for support review according to the SLA and escalation policy.
5. Escalate immediately if the customer reports a missing delivery or the shipment records conflict with the customer's evidence.
