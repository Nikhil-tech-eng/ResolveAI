# Fraud and Unauthorized Charges

1. Acknowledge the report calmly and treat an unknown charge as a security-sensitive issue.
2. Do not ask for a full card number, password, security code, or one-time authentication code.
3. Record only the minimum transaction details needed to identify the report through approved tools.
4. Do not promise a refund, identify a perpetrator, or make account changes unless verified records and an authorized policy permit it.
5. Escalate immediately to the fraud-support queue and explain that a specialist will review the report.
