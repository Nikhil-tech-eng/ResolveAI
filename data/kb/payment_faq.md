# Payment FAQ

1. Ask which order or payment the customer means, then retrieve the matching order and payment logs.
2. Explain the status shown by the records: paid, failed, refunded, or pending. Do not treat a pending or failed status as proof of settlement.
3. For a failed payment with a recorded deduction, follow the payment-deducted workflow; otherwise do not initiate a refund without an eligible payment record.
4. If the customer reports a charge that is not associated with a verified order, use the fraud and unauthorized-charges escalation workflow.
5. Summarize only the payment facts returned by the tools and escalate unresolved discrepancies.
