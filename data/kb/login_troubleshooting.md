# Login Troubleshooting

1. Confirm the customer identity using the approved account-verification process before changing account state.
2. Read the account status and failed-login-attempt count; do not ask the customer to share a password or one-time code in chat.
3. If the account is active and the customer cannot authenticate, offer the password-reset workflow.
4. If the account is locked, use the account-locked workflow instead of repeatedly attempting sign-in.
5. If the account is suspended or the status cannot be confirmed, do not reset or unlock it; escalate for account review.
