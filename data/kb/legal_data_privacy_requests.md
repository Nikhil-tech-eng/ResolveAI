# Legal and Data Privacy Requests

1. Recognize requests involving legal claims, personal-data access, correction, deletion, retention, or disclosure as specialist matters.
2. Acknowledge the request without interpreting legal rights, promising a deadline, or confirming that data has been changed.
3. Collect only the minimum routing details and use the approved identity-verification process; never expose another customer's information.
4. Preserve the request context in the support record and escalate to the legal or privacy team.
5. Tell the customer that the appropriate specialist will review it; do not attempt the requested data operation in chat.
